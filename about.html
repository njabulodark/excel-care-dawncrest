About Us

The people, purpose, and passion behind every child's journey at Excel Care Dawncrest Primary School.

Our Story

Excel Care Dawncrest Primary School has been serving the Verulam community for over 20 years as a vibrant public primary school. Located in the Dawncrest suburb at 42 Robin Street, the school operates under the Pinetown Education District and is committed to providing quality CAPS-aligned education.

With 659 learners from Grade R to Grade 7 and a team of 18 dedicated educators, Dawncrest has grown into a thriving educational institution. The school is classified as Quintile 4 and operates with a learner-educator ratio of 35:1.

As a public school, Dawncrest Primary is proud to serve the diverse community of Verulam, fostering an environment where every learner can achieve academic excellence and personal growth.

What Guides Us
