Academics

A thoughtfully designed curriculum that sparks wonder, builds confidence, and lays strong foundations for lifelong learning.

Learning at Dawncrest

Our learners engage with the CAPS curriculum in well-equipped classrooms with modern teaching resources. From Foundation Phase literacy and numeracy to Senior Phase sciences and technology, every lesson is designed to inspire curiosity and achievement.

>

Learning Through
Wonder

Our curriculum is inspired by the Reggio Emilia approach — a philosophy that views children as capable, resourceful learners who construct knowledge through exploration and meaningful relationships.

Rather than rigid lesson plans, our teachers follow the children's interests, weaving academic foundations into projects that emerge organically. Nature, art, and conversation are the primary tools; wonder is the driving force.

Every classroom is designed as a "third teacher" — an environment rich with provocations, natural materials, and open-ended possibilities that invite inquiry and imaginative thinking.

Child-Led Exploration

Children choose their learning path while teachers gently guide, scaffold, and document their discoveries.

Nature Integration

Our sensory gardens, outdoor classrooms, and nature walks connect children to the living world every day.

Creative Expression

Art, music, drama, and storytelling are woven into every day, cultivating the hundred languages of children.

Our Phases

Three thoughtfully designed pathways, each tailored to a child's developmental stage.

Grades R–3

Foundation Phase

Our youngest learners thrive in a warm, home-like environment designed for sensory-rich exploration. Through gentle routines and hands-on discovery, toddlers build the language, motor, and social skills that form the bedrock of all future learning.

Sensory play stations with natural materials
Early language through songs, rhymes & storytelling
Fine & gross motor skill development
Gentle social skill building & emotional regulation

Enrichment Programs

Integrated into the curriculum — not add-ons — these programmes deepen every child's experience.

STEM Lab

Weekly experiments, robotics basics, and engineering challenges spark scientific curiosity from the very start.

Rhythm, percussion, singing, and creative dance develop coordination, expression, and an early love of the arts.

Immersive French and Mandarin sessions through stories, games, and songs build multilingual foundations naturally.
