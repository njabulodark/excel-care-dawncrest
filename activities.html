Activities

From art and music to gardening and STEM — discover the diverse experiences that enrich every child's journey at Dawncrest Primary.

Beyond the Classroom

At Dawncrest Primary, we believe learning extends beyond textbooks. Our extracurricular programmes in arts, music, sport, and cultural activities help every learner discover their talents and build confidence.

>

What We Offer

Six enrichment programs designed to spark curiosity, build confidence, and develop the whole child.

Creative Arts

Painting, sculpting, mixed-media crafts, and textile art that nurture self-expression and fine motor development.

Grades R–7

Music

Singing, rhythm instruments, creative dance, and movement games that build coordination and musical awareness.

Grades R–7

Nature & Gardening

Planting seeds, nature walks, composting, and outdoor exploration that connect children with the natural world.

Ages 3–6

Cooking & Nutrition

Simple recipes, healthy eating habits, measuring, and mixing — learning maths and science through food preparation.

Ages 3–6

STEM Discovery

Hands-on experiments, building challenges, and coding basics that foster logical thinking and problem-solving skills.

Ages 4–6

Drama & Storytelling

Puppet shows, role-play, reader's theatre, and creative storytelling that build language, empathy, and confidence.

Ages 3–6
