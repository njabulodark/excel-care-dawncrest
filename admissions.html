How to Enroll

Step 4

Welcome Day

Your child is officially part of the family! We celebrate with a special welcome ceremony.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about enrolling at Dawncrest Primary.

We welcome children aged 2 to 6 years. Our three programs — Foundation Phase (2–3), Senior Phase (3–4), and Life Skills & Sport (5–6) — are designed to meet each developmental stage with age-appropriate learning and care.
Our regular school hours are Monday to Friday, 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Core learning takes place from 8:00 AM to 1:00 PM, with supervised extended care available until 6:00 PM. Saturday mornings from 8:00 AM to 12:00 PM are available for optional enrichment activities.
Yes! All our programs include nutritious meals prepared on-site by our kitchen team. We serve morning tea, a balanced lunch, and an afternoon snack. We accommodate dietary requirements and allergies — just let us know during enrollment.
We maintain intentionally small class sizes. Our ratios are 1:6 for Foundation Phase (ages 2–3), 1:8 for Senior Phase (ages 3–4), and 1:10 for Life Skills & Sport (ages 5–6). Each class also has a dedicated teaching assistant.
Absolutely. We offer a complimentary two-day trial period so your child can experience our classroom environment before committing. This also gives our teachers a chance to understand your child's personality and needs. Parents are welcome to observe.
We follow the standard South African school calendar with four terms. Major holidays include two weeks in December/January, one week in March/April, one week in June/July, and one week in September/October. A detailed calendar is shared with families at the start of each year.
